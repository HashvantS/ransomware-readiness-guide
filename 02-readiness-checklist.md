# Ransomware Readiness Checklist

Tick each item that is **fully true today**. One tick = 1 point (25 total).

## A. Backups
- [ ] Critical data is copied in 3 places (original + 2 backups)
- [ ] Backups use at least 2 different media (e.g. cloud + external disk)
- [ ] At least 1 copy is offsite, offline or immutable
- [ ] Backups run automatically
- [ ] A restore test was done in the last 90 days and the result written down

## B. Access and accounts
- [ ] Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is on email and admin accounts
- [ ] MFA is on any remote-access tool
- [ ] Each person has their own login
- [ ] Staff use normal accounts daily, not administrator accounts
- [ ] Accounts of people who left were removed within a week

## C. Updates and protection
- [ ] Operating systems and apps update automatically or on a fixed schedule
- [ ] Router/firewall firmware is up to date
- [ ] Anti-malware/endpoint protection is active on every computer
- [ ] Unused software and remote-access services are switched off
- [ ] Email filtering for spam and malicious attachments is on

## D. People
- [ ] Staff had phishing awareness training in the last 12 months
- [ ] Staff know exactly who to tell about a suspicious email
- [ ] Reporting a mistake is encouraged, not punished

## E. Detection and response
- [ ] An incident lead (and deputy) is named
- [ ] A printed contact list exists (IT support, insurer, police/cyber cell, vendors)
- [ ] A written recovery plan exists and staff have read it
- [ ] The plan was practised once in a short tabletop exercise

## F. Legal and insurance
- [ ] You know which data you hold and where it lives
- [ ] You know your incident-reporting duties (CERT-In, data protection law) **[VERIFY]**
- [ ] You have checked whether cyber insurance is worth having

## Scoring
| Score | Readiness | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Low | Fix backups, MFA and updates first |
| 11–19 | Medium | Close gaps in People and Response; practise the plan |
| 20–25 | High | Keep testing restores; review every 6 months |
