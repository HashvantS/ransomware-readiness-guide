## 2. First 60 minutes
- [ ] Note the time and what was seen (ransom note, locked files, odd behaviour)
- [ ] Disconnect affected devices from the network; do not wipe or reformat
- [ ] Phone the incident lead and IT support; do not use email from affected devices
- [ ] Photograph the screen and ransom note
- [ ] Stop staff from using shared systems until told
- [ ] Check whether backups are intact and disconnected

## 3. Next 24 hours
