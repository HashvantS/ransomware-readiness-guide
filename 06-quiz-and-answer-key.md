# Quiz (10 questions)

1. What does ransomware do to a business's files?
   - A. Deletes them permanently  B. Encrypts them and demands payment  C. Copies them to the cloud  D. Speeds up the computer
2. What does the 3-2-1 backup rule mean?
   - A. 3 passwords, 2 locks, 1 guard  B. 3 copies, 2 media types, 1 offsite or offline  C. 3 backups a day, 2 a week, 1 a month  D. 3 users, 2 admins, 1 owner
3. Which is a common way ransomware gets in?
   - A. A phishing email  B. A power cut  C. A software licence  D. A printer cartridge
4. Why should one backup copy be offline or unchangeable?
   - A. It is cheaper  B. Attackers try to encrypt backups they can reach  C. It makes backups faster  D. Laws require it
5. What is double extortion?
   - A. Asking for payment twice  B. Locking files and also threatening to publish stolen data  C. Attacking two businesses  D. Using two types of malware
6. Which control adds a second check beyond a password?
   - A. Antivirus  B. A firewall  C. Multi-factor authentication  D. A screen saver
7. You see a ransom message on your computer. What should you do first?
   - A. Restart it  B. Pay quickly to avoid delay  C. Disconnect from the network and tell the incident lead  D. Delete the message
8. Why is a backup you have never tested risky?
   - A. It takes more space  B. It may fail when you need it  C. It costs more  D. It cannot be encrypted
9. Which statement about paying a ransom is most accurate?
   - A. Paying always returns your data  B. Paying is illegal everywhere  C. Payment gives no guarantee, and it is a decision to take with professional and legal advice  D. Paying removes the vulnerability
10. (Scenario) A colleague emails asking you to urgently buy gift cards and says not to tell anyone. What do you do?
    - A. Buy them quickly  B. Reply asking for details  C. Verify by phone using a known number and report it  D. Forward it to the team

---

# Answer Key

| Q | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | B | Ransomware encrypts files and demands payment for the key |
| 2 | B | Three copies, on two media types, with one offsite or offline |
| 3 | A | Phishing emails are among the most common entry points |
| 4 | B | Connected backups can be encrypted along with the main data |
| 5 | B | Data is locked and also threatened with leak |
| 6 | C | MFA requires a second proof of identity |
| 7 | C | Isolating limits spread; the lead starts the plan |
| 8 | B | Untested backups often fail or are incomplete |
| 9 | C | No guarantee; take professional and legal advice |
| 10 | C | Urgent, secret payment requests are a classic scam sign; verify through a known channel |
