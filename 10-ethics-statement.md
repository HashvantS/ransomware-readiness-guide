# Ethics Statement

- This project uses a **fictional business** (Sunrise Family Clinic). No real organisation, person or incident is described.
- It contains **no malware code, samples or instructions** for carrying out attacks. AI tools were asked for defensive guidance only.
- Advice on **ransom payment** is presented as uncertain and case-dependent, never as a rule. Readers are directed to professional and legal advice.
- All statistics, legal duties and technical claims generated by AI were **checked against official sources**. Errors, outdated points and unsafe suggestions are recorded in the [verification report](07-verification-report.md).
- A **human reviewed** every deliverable before submission.
- AI tools used and their roles are listed in the README. Any text adapted from sources is cited.
